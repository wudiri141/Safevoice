Resources

Helpful guidance before and after reporting.

These tips help users prepare a clearer report and understand what to do after submitting it.

Before you report

  • Gather dates, times, and exact locations
  • Note names, vehicle details, or descriptions if possible
  • Save screenshots, photos, or witness notes
  • Use the closest report type if you are unsure

After you report

  • Keep your reference information in a safe place
  • Be ready for possible follow-up questions
  • Contact emergency services if the situation escalates
  • Share only what is necessary and safe to disclose
