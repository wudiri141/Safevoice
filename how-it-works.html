How It Works

Simple steps from report to response.

Each stage is visible so users understand what happens after they submit information.

1

Report

Share what you know with details, locations, and evidence if available.

2

Review

The report is checked and categorized so urgent cases can be prioritized.

3

Respond

Partner teams and trusted agencies can take action where needed.

4

Protect

SafeVoice aims to help communities become safer through timely action.

What a good report includes

  • Date and time of the incident
  • Exact location or closest landmark
  • Names, descriptions, or vehicles if known
  • Images, screenshots, or witness notes

Need urgent help?

If there is immediate danger, use emergency services first. SafeVoice is for reporting and follow-up support, not a replacement for emergency response.

SafeVoice helps citizens anonymously report abuse, trafficking and suspicious activities.
