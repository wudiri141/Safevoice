Report Types

Choose the right category for the report.

Matching the incident to the correct category helps the report move to the right support path faster.

Child Abuse

Physical, emotional or sexual abuse.

Child Trafficking

Sale, recruitment or exploitation.

Drug Trafficking

Illegal drug production or sales.

Human Trafficking

Forced labour, exploitation or servitude.

Child Labour

Illegal or hazardous work by children.

Neglect

Abandonment or failure to care.

When you are not sure

Pick the closest category and provide a clear explanation in the report. The review process can route the case from there.
