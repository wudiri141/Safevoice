About SafeVoice

Built to feel serious, calm, and trustworthy.

SafeVoice helps citizens anonymously report abuse, trafficking, and suspicious activity using a clean structure that works on desktop and mobile without feeling crowded.

📝

Report with confidence

Share details clearly, including what happened, where it happened, and what you observed.

🛡️

Trusted review

Your report is reviewed through a structured process so urgent cases can be prioritized.

👥

Take action

Partner agencies and support teams can respond to protect victims and reduce harm.

❤️

Protect lives

Communities become safer when people speak up early and report suspicious activity.

Report Types

What can you report?

These categories match the original SafeVoice idea and help guide each report to the right response path.

Child Abuse

Physical, emotional or sexual abuse.

Child Trafficking

Sale, recruitment or exploitation.

Drug Trafficking

Illegal drug production or sales.

Human Trafficking

Forced labour, exploitation or servitude.

Child Labour

Illegal or hazardous work by children.

Neglect

Abandonment or failure to care.
